How to code each video

Watch the first 60-90 seconds. Read the title and description below the video. You do NOT need to watch the full video.

Question 1: humor

YES — the video tries to make you smile or laugh through:

  • Jokes, puns, or wordplay
  • Exaggerated or silly situations
  • Parody or making fun of something
  • Sarcasm, irony, or unexpected mismatches
  • Visual or verbal comedy timing
  • Warm, playful, or lighthearted framing with comedic intent

The video does NOT need to be funny to you — only to be trying to be funny.

NO — the video is serious or informational, with no playful or comedic framing.

Question 1a: humor style (pick one)

StyleOne-line definition
AFFILIATIVE Humor used to bring people together — jokes, banter, or wit that creates social bonding between the speaker and the audience. No one is the target.
SELF-ENHANCING Humor as a positive outlook on life — finding the funny side of difficulties, amusement at life’s absurdities, often conveying resilience or optimism.
AGGRESSIVE Humor at someone’s expense — sarcasm, ridicule, mocking competitors or types of people. The humor has a target.
SELF-DEFEATING Humor at one’s own expense to gain acceptance or connection — putting yourself or your brand down, ingratiating self-deprecation.

Question 2: empowerment

YES — the video shows AI giving someone (a person or a firm) any of these:

  • new ability to do things (operational)
  • new ways to create or express (creative)
  • more control or choice (agentic)
  • new ways to act on their values (normative)

Quick test: will someone gain ability, creativity, control, or a values-aligned outcome from this AI?

NO — pure technology talk, business or industry news, or AI mentions with no one shown benefiting.

Question 2a: who is empowered (pick one)

Binary choice — the framework distinguishes humans from corporations.

CategoryOne-line definition
INDIVIDUAL Any human being — a consumer, a worker, an artist, a citizen, anyone with a face and a name.
FIRM A company, corporation, or brand as a whole entity — the firm selling AI, the firm buying AI, or the brand itself.

Edge cases: a government delivering services is acting like a firm (institutional actor); citizens benefiting from those services are individuals. Society or "the public" usually means a collective of individuals.

Question 2b: type of empowerment (pick one)

TypeOne-line definitionCue words
OPERATIONAL AI helps with familiar tasks — faster, easier, less effort. save time, automate, faster, streamline, understanding, literacy
CREATIVE AI helps make something new — art, designs, writing, music, ideas. create, generate, imagine, design
AGENTIC AI gives more control, choice, or autonomy. you decide, your way, customize, take control, autonomous, autonomy
NORMATIVE AI helps act on values — ethics, fairness, sustainability, safety, inclusion. responsible, trustworthy, ethical, sustainable
