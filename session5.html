How to code each video

Watch the first 60-90 seconds. Read the title and description below the video. You do NOT need to watch the full video.

Question 1: humor

YES — the video tries to make you smile or laugh through:

  • Jokes, puns, or wordplay
  • Exaggerated or silly situations
  • Parody or making fun of something
  • Sarcasm, irony, or unexpected mismatches
  • Visual or verbal comedy timing

The video does NOT need to be funny to you — only to be trying to be funny.

NO — the video is serious, informational, or just warmly delivered without any comedy.

Question 1a: humor style (pick one)

StyleOne-line definition
AFFILIATIVE Warm, inclusive humor that brings people together; no one is the target.
SELF-ENHANCING Humor that finds the funny side of life's challenges without putting anyone down.
AGGRESSIVE Humor at someone’s expense — sarcasm, ridicule, mocking competitors or types of people.
SELF-DEFEATING The speaker or brand is the butt of the joke — self-deprecation, "we’re not that great" framing.

Question 2: empowerment

YES — the video shows AI giving someone (a person or a firm) any of these:

  • new ability to do things (operational)
  • new ways to create or express (creative)
  • more control or choice (agentic)
  • new ways to act on their values (normative)

Quick test: will someone gain ability, creativity, control, or a values-aligned outcome from this AI?

NO — pure technology talk, business or industry news, or AI mentions with no one shown benefiting.

UNSURE — use only when you cannot decide between yes and no.

Question 2a: who is empowered (pick one)

CategoryOne-line definition
INDIVIDUAL Any human being — a consumer, a worker, an artist, anyone with a face and a name.
FIRM A company, corporation, or brand as a whole entity.
OTHERS The empowered party is something else — government, society at large, the public.

Question 2b: type of empowerment (pick one)

TypeOne-line definitionCue words
OPERATIONAL AI helps with familiar tasks — faster, easier, less effort. save time, automate, faster, streamline
CREATIVE AI helps make something new — art, designs, writing, music, ideas. create, generate, imagine, design
AGENTIC AI gives more control, choice, or autonomy. you decide, your way, customize, take control
NORMATIVE AI helps act on values — ethics, fairness, sustainability, safety, inclusion. responsible, trustworthy, ethical, sustainable

Remember

  • Humor and empowerment are separate questions. A video can have neither, one, or both.
  • If a video doesn’t clearly fit a category, use UNSURE or OTHERS rather than guessing.
