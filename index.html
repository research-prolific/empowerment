Coding Study — humor & AI empowerment in corporate videos

Coding webapp for Prolific participants

Welcome — thank you for taking part

You will code corporate YouTube videos on two questions: whether the video uses humor, and whether it shows AI empowering someone. The task is divided into 5 sessions that you can complete in one sitting or spread across the day. Your answers auto-save.

About this study. Your coding directly supports academic research on how companies communicate about AI. The dataset you help create will be used in a peer-reviewed academic paper and will inform future research on AI marketing and empowerment.

Different coders work through the same videos, and the consistency between you is what lets the dataset be trusted. We count on you to take this seriously. Thank you.

Tip: a "View codebook" button is available below and at the top of every coding page — click it any time you need a refresher on the categories.
How to code each video: Watch the first ~60-90 seconds, then read the title and description shown below the video. You do NOT need to watch each video in full — corporate AI marketing videos establish their humor framing and empowerment claims in the first minute.

QuestionAnswerWhy
1. Does this video use humor? Yes The whole video is a comedy bit, narrated by a struggling comedian. The title "Comedy Coach", the description's "flex your funny bone", and the #Comedy hashtag all signal comedic intent.
1a. Humor style Self-defeating The comedian is the butt of the jokes — dreaming of their "headshot on the wall of pizza shops", being told to give up by their inner "evil me" voice, playing Brooklyn basements that "smell not good". The humor mocks the speaker themselves.
2. Does AI empower someone? Yes Copilot is shown giving the comedian new ability — generating a step-by-step roadmap of comedy career advice, and creating mood board images of the comedian succeeding (theater stage, late-night show appearance).
2a. Who is empowered? Individual The comedian is one person pursuing a personal career dream. They are an aspiring artist working solo — not a company.
2b. Type of empowerment Creative The description explicitly says Copilot "helps you visualize your dreams with custom mood boards". The AI generates novel visual content (mood board images of theaters and late-night shows) that the comedian could not produce alone. This is creating something new — the defining mark of creative empowerment.
Notice how much is in the title and description. The title "Comedy Coach" + the description's "flex your funny bone" + the #Comedy hashtag already tell you humor=yes. The description's "visualize your dreams with custom mood boards" already tells you empowerment=yes and type=creative. Watching the first minute of the video confirms the framing and tells you the empowered party is an individual (the comedian). This is the workflow you'll repeat for every video.

Codebook

Quick reference for each coding question. Stick to these definitions — the goal is for different coders to agree on the same video.

How to code each video

Watch the first 60-90 seconds. Read the title and description below the video. You do NOT need to watch the full video.

Question 1: humor

YES — the video tries to make you smile or laugh through:

  • Jokes, puns, or wordplay
  • Exaggerated or silly situations
  • Parody or making fun of something
  • Sarcasm, irony, or unexpected mismatches
  • Visual or verbal comedy timing

The video does NOT need to be funny to you — only to be trying to be funny.

NO — the video is serious, informational, or just warmly delivered without any comedy.

Question 1a: humor style (pick one)

StyleOne-line definition
AFFILIATIVE Warm, inclusive humor that brings people together; no one is the target.
SELF-ENHANCING Humor that finds the funny side of life's challenges without putting anyone down.
AGGRESSIVE Humor at someone’s expense — sarcasm, ridicule, mocking competitors or types of people.
SELF-DEFEATING The speaker or brand is the butt of the joke — self-deprecation, "we’re not that great" framing.

Question 2: empowerment

YES — the video shows AI giving someone (a person or a firm) any of these:

  • new ability to do things (operational)
  • new ways to create or express (creative)
  • more control or choice (agentic)
  • new ways to act on their values (normative)

Quick test: will someone gain ability, creativity, control, or a values-aligned outcome from this AI?

NO — pure technology talk, business or industry news, or AI mentions with no one shown benefiting.

Question 2a: who is empowered (pick one)

CategoryOne-line definition
INDIVIDUAL Any human being — a consumer, a worker, an artist, anyone with a face and a name.
FIRM A company, corporation, or brand as a whole entity.
OTHERS The empowered party is something else — government, society at large, the public.

Question 2b: type of empowerment (pick one)

TypeOne-line definitionCue words
OPERATIONAL AI helps with familiar tasks — faster, easier, less effort. save time, automate, faster, streamline
CREATIVE AI helps make something new — art, designs, writing, music, ideas. create, generate, imagine, design
AGENTIC AI gives more control, choice, or autonomy. you decide, your way, customize, take control
NORMATIVE AI helps act on values — ethics, fairness, sustainability, safety, inclusion. responsible, trustworthy, ethical, sustainable

Remember

  • Humor and empowerment are separate questions. A video can have neither, one, or both.
  • If a video doesn’t clearly fit Individual or Firm, use OTHERS rather than guessing.
